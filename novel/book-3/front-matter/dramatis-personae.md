# Dramatis Personae — Book Three

*Characters carried forward from Books One and Two are described here as who this book finds them. See [Book One's](../../book-1/front-matter/dramatis-personae.md) and [Book Two's](../../book-2/front-matter/dramatis-personae.md) lists for their earlier shape.*

**CAELEN** — a decade on from the mountain, and no longer able to be forgotten. Spent nine years learning to hide well. Spends this book learning what it costs to be seen, and finding it is a different sort of debt than he expected.

**ROOK** — the same fast tongue, slower now to use it. Has learned that staying is a skill, and has become, without anyone noticing, the one the others lean on.

**SERAPHINA** — archivist of the rite, and daughter of a House that survived her brother by forgetting him. Comes home in this book to be asked, politely, to help it go on forgetting.

**MALAKAR** — once Inquisitor-Commander, now a man with a name on a public record. Spends eleven years of precise habit on the first sentence in his life that no doctrine can defend.

**ORELL** — a Warden captain who chose, late and at cost, which of his two loyalties had been the real one.

**CORWIN** — second son of a lower-ring House, mender of one cuff, keeper of a green door. Has been careful his whole life, and finds in this book that the reckless thing was the only thing he was ever for.

**LISBET** — his sister. Nineteen, drifting once, better now. Refuses to be carried into a hall or a ward, and walks.

**ILSE** and **ODO** — archivists of the camp, one young and freckled and tired past bearing, one stooped and patient. The rite's first students, and its first honest critics.

---

*In memoriam*

**ANSEL**, forge-keeper of Hollow Cut. **VARRICK**, scavenger, liar about his own motives. **FENN**, brass-fitter, who finished it properly.

## New to This Book's Ledger

**CHAIRMAN RETH** — the Mining Guild's ruling Board, in a plain grey coat. Forty years keeping a second book in a locked drawer for the dead nobody else would count. Not greedy. Right about a great deal, which is what makes him dangerous.

**CUSTODIAN MAREN** — twenty-nine, acting Eldest by accident, afraid in a robe. Says *I do not know* aloud, which the Synod has not heard in three generations.

**LORD ILVANE** — Seraphina's father. Has never raised his voice. Has never, until now, said his son's name.

**HALLORAN** — steward of House Ilvane. Carried the trays to a door he was not allowed to open, and has never been able to forget it.

**CAPTAIN ROSHE** — Warden, Kesh Hollow garrison. Signed a mother's death in the passive voice, and was promoted for it.

**PETRA HALSEY** — clerk of the Long Reach garrison. Misfiled thirty-one orders. Has the ink-stained fingers of a woman who writes things down.
