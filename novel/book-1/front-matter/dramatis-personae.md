# Dramatis Personae

**CAELEN** — a boy pulled living from a sealed stratum no survey ever charted. Not Lithoi, though something in him remembers being grown rather than born. What he is has no clause in any doctrine yet written.

**VARRICK** — a scavenger who deals in things other men are too frightened to carry, including, eventually, a child. Does not do charity. Insists on this more than the evidence of his own life supports.

**ROOK** — of Hollow Cut, fast-talking, fiercely loyal, more afraid of losing people than of anything that has ever tried to kill him. Usually both at once.

**MALAKAR** — a Warden Inquisitor who has built a career, and a conscience, on the belief that precise language is the only clean thing left in his work. The doctrine has not yet written a clause for what he is being asked to hunt.

**SERAPHINA** — of the Spire, once. Carries her exile the way court-trained people carry everything: formally, and only ever slipping where she forgets to hold the shape of it.

---

*In memoriam*

**ANSEL** — forge-keeper of Hollow Cut. Fed strays without asking their names first. Spent exactly what he meant to, and no more.

## The Factions

**THE MINING GUILD** — owns the deep strata and everything pulled living or dead out of it. Keeps a ledger. The ledger does not note ages.

**THE WARDEN ENFORCERS** — police the unlicensed trade in Aethel-Cores and the men who Shift without paying for the privilege first. Prefer to call their violence *procedure*.

**THE SYNOD** — adjudicates what the Lithoi remains are owed, morally, by the living who profit from them. Answers most questions with another question, and calls the habit humility.
