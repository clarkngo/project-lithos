# Dramatis Personae — Book Two

*Characters carried forward from Book One are described here as who this book finds them, not as who they were when it began. See [Book One's dramatis personae](../../book-1/front-matter/dramatis-personae.md) for their earlier shape.*

**CAELEN** — nine years on from the mountain, and steadier for it. Spends the discipline that once only saved his own life keeping other people's bodies from being spent past what they can afford. Still not claimed by anyone who's bothered writing it down.

**VARRICK** — older, greyer, slower rising in the mornings, the Vault's own wound never healed the way any of them let themselves hope. Spends this book's early chapters still insisting, out of habit more than conviction, that none of it was ever more than arithmetic. Stops insisting, once, near the end, and means every word of the admission. His ledger closes within these pages. It does not close cleanly, and nobody who loved him would have wanted it any other way.

**ROOK** — steadier now, the old fast rhythm spent more carefully, though it still finds its footing fastest at a found-family fire. Learns, this book, the difference between fear that runs and fear that stays and fights anyway.

**MALAKAR** — formally renounced by the Warden order, and building, quietly, one careful name at a time, the thing that order has never once had: a conscience working from the inside. Finds, this book, that eleven years of doctrine can still be spent on something worth spending it on.

**SERAPHINA** — the reliquary's fugitive archivist, now its most dangerous unlicensed teacher. Carries the tethering rite out of one private grief and into a season's worth of families who'd been told, the same way she was, that nothing could be done. Learns exactly what that mercy costs, and pays it anyway.

**FENN** — a brass-fitter first, a Drift-afflicted stranger second, though neither description holds him whole for very long. Walked three weeks to ask for a season he was told, honestly, the rite couldn't fully buy him. Spends what he's given protecting people who never once asked him to.

**CAPTAIN ORELL** — of the Warden order, formerly. Spent eleven years taking Malakar's reports without comment. Spends this book finally deciding what all that careful silence actually meant, and what it's going to cost him to stop keeping it.

---

*In memoriam*

**ANSEL** — forge-keeper of Hollow Cut. Remembered still, in this book, by a boy who promised to carry the memory longer than he carried the fire.

**VARRICK** — scavenger, liar about his own motives, father in every sense but the word neither of them was ever quite brave enough to use first. Nine years a debt. Never once, in all that time, actually charity.

## New to This Book's Ledger

**DIRECTOR KELL** — Mining Guild, Eastern Territories Extraction. Reads her predecessor's failure as a failure of method, not of goal, and builds her whole approach on patience the last Director never had the discipline to spend. Considers her price honest. Isn't wrong, exactly, and that's the trouble with her.

**CUSTODIAN VOSS** — the Synod's answer to a doctrine it no longer fully believes in. Built thirty years of certainty out of a single afternoon's grief, and cannot, even now, entirely tell the difference between the certainty and the wound underneath it. Not a villain. A believer, which is very often worse.
