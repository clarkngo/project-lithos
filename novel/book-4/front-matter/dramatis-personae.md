# Dramatis Personae — Book Four

*Characters carried forward from earlier books are described here as who this book finds them. See the [Book Three list](../../book-3/front-matter/dramatis-personae.md) for their earlier shape.*

**CAELEN** — a decade and more on from the mountain, and sailing, for the first time, toward a child in the amber instead of away from one. Spends the book learning that the thing he has banked every hour of his life was never an enemy, and what it costs to set it down.

**ROOK** — the same fast tongue, seasick for twenty-two days and still the first to say the true thing plainly. Pays for this book in one ear.

**SERAPHINA** — archivist of the rite. Finds out what it was always for, and that three books of work were exactly right and only half the song.

**MALAKAR** — reads a Guild appendix the way he once read a case file, for what the language was built to hide.

**CORWIN** — second son, mender of one cuff, first-time sailor. Learns what the sea looks like when it does not end, and what a wrist is for.

**THE GIRL** — nine years old or thereabouts, found sealed in honey stone, awake and without a name or a language anyone could hear. Chooses both for herself.

**TAMSIN ORRE** — tide-clerk of the third harbor, twenty-two, keeper of a pencil log inside her sash. The first thing the girl saw. Learns, late, that carrying the tray was not the same as sitting down.

**ONDREI VESH** — Harbor-Master and Keeper of the Deep Ledger. Dressed in his best coat for a hearing he was afraid of. Spent nineteen years keeping the dead before learning he had been keeping a nursery.

**BRINE-REEVE HASK** — head of the harbor council and keeper of the door. Not a cruel man, a careful one, and afraid, with reason, of being wrong.

**CLERK-SUPERIOR IBB** — seventy, bone-thin, taught Tamsin her letters. Wanted the sleepers holy and found they were only alive.

**NESSA** — keeper of twenty-two lamps. Her mother kept them before her.

---

*In memoriam*

**ANSEL**, forge-keeper of Hollow Cut. **VARRICK**, scavenger, who did not look back. **FENN**, brass-fitter, who finished it properly. **YSOLT MARR**, tide-clerk, dismissed for conduct, who sat with a sleeper and hummed.

## New to This Book's Ledger

**CHAIRMAN RETH** *(returning)* — still in a plain grey coat, still right about the numbers. Sails to the Salt Coast to stop a charge he has been told he cannot stop.

**ENGINEER-CAPTAIN HALSE** — square, sandy, courteous, sincere. Disobeys the way a very reasonable man does, and is the first to ask to be locked up.
