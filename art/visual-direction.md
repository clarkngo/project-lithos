# Visual direction

Working art bible for *The Last Lithoi*. Confirm against the manuscript when prose and image disagree; patch this file, not the chapter.

## Illustration style (locked)

**Late-90s JRPG box art / painted-cel** — the `explorations/jrpg-v2/` pass. That language is the Codex's main art: current keepers in `world/`, `characters/`, `covers/`, `lithoi/`, `artifacts/`, and `resonance/`.

Take from that pass:

- Painted-cel party posters, overworld paintings, inventory-clear props
- Saturated but earthy (charcoal, brass, dirty cloth, amber-dark glass)
- Thumbnail-clear silhouettes; character-forward
- Original designs only — no publisher marks, no fake studio logos, no dated “coming soon” paste-on type
- Character portraits have **no overlay text**. Captions belong in the Codex. If a portrait has a stamp, regenerate it. Poster titles are allowed on the series key-art and on volume covers in `art/covers/`. Volume covers are **places** (the seam, the camp and second seal, the Central Hall), not party lineups. The series key-art stays the Codex home poster.

Do **not** take from that pass (or from Breath of Fire): extra blonde party members, hero swords, green/blue spiky hair, crystal-goddess Lithoi, majestic western dragons, floating castle-temples.

Other explorations stay archived for reference: `cinematic-v1`, `cel-jrpg-v1`, `bande-dessinee-v1`. Do not mix their drawing languages into new pieces unless explicitly testing.

## JRPG rules for this book

- Cast on a piece = only people who exist on the page. No invented party.
- Caelen (Act One): nine, unarmed, dusty **dark** hair, thin shift, cat-eye lantern shine, caloric-debt thin.
- Varrick: scavenger, pry-bar, pack — not a knight.
- A Shift behind the party is allowed (that's the genre kinship) but it must look **costly**: torn joints, gaunt, dull marrow-glass in the ribs — not a clean dragon.
- Lithoi reconstructions are **people**, terrene and dense, marrow inside the bone. Not statues, not crystal fairies, no plaques.

## What the world should feel like

The Lithoi world is felt through the body before it is understood through the mind. Even in painted-cel, pictures should carry **temperature, weight, dust, and oil**.

- **Industrial-sacred**, not crystal-kingdom high fantasy.
- **Aftermath light.** Lantern orange, overcast grey-green, marrow-glass as dark amber — a held coal, not materia-neon.
- **Cost on the surface.** Gauntness, cracked nails, wet cloth, brass that reads cold.

## Palette

| Role | Painted-cel notes |
|---|---|
| Stone ink | Near-black, wet rock, unlit gallery |
| Brass | Worn ochre-brass, not jewelry-gold |
| Marrow / amber | Amber-dark glass, honey in shadow. Small held glow, never neon |
| Loam / forest | Green-black, dripping |
| Wastes | Bleached ochre, bottle-glass, ash |
| Paper / skin | Cold, underfed; lantern on faces |

## Materials (get these right)

- **Marrow-glass / Aethel-Cores:** vitrified *organ and marrow*, not gemstones. Glassy, amber-dark, faintly warm, veined like fossil tissue.
- **Brass conduit:** forearm housing, **three slots**, straps and oil, always brass. Cold before a Shift, scalding after. No runes, no LED traces.
- **Lithoi (illustrative):** humanoid-terrene — people, not golems, not crystal folk, not elves. Density is the point. Extinction reads as grief.
- **Human kit:** work-worn mining and scavenger cloth, not JRPG hero capes unless the page earns them (it has not).

## Hard bans

- Copying Breath of Fire (or any other game) character designs, creatures, or wardrobe
- Inventing extra party members for a prettier poster
- Clean or empowering Shifts (no costume-change werewolves, no superhero muscle-up)
- Glowing magic runes, UI glyphs, “mana”
- Apostrophe-fantasy names on props or signage
- Cute mascot Lithoi or living Lithoi in the present (they are extinct)

## Character locks (from the page)

- **Caelen (Act One, child):** nine, just unsealed from amber-dark glass; joints unused to length; thin shift; palms that knit too warm; eyes that throw lantern-light back like a cat’s; thinning wrists, grey lips. Hair is **not** a blue or green hero spike — mine-dusted, dark, uneven.
- **Caelen (Act Two onward):** the same person grown — taller than Varrick, lean Wastes runner, dusty dark hair, cat-eye lantern catchlights. Empty brass housing on the **left** forearm, three empty slots, never held a core. Unarmed.
- **Varrick:** not young; grey-threaded beard trimmed with more care than the rest of him; pack straps worn pale; short iron pry-bar; oilcloth bundle that sits faintly warm.

Rook, Malakar, and Seraphina have **no confirmed likeness** yet. Kell, Voss, Fenn, Orell, Reth, Ilvane, Maren, and Corwin have working portraits in `art/characters/`.

## How to brief a new piece

1. Prepend style lock E (late-90s JRPG painted-cel). Optionally attach `explorations/jrpg-v2/` keepers as *style* references.
2. Name the Lithos subject and beat.
3. List materials (brass, marrow-glass, stone dust, rain).
4. State who is allowed in frame — and who is not.
5. Mark `illustrative` if the manuscript has not confirmed it.
