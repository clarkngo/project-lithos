# Prompts

Default lock is **E**. Other locks are archive-only. Never copy Breath of Fire (or any game) character designs. Never invent extra party members.

## Style lock E — late-90s JRPG (default)

Late 1990s JRPG promotional illustration, painted-cel. Saturated but earthy: charcoal, worn brass, dirty cloth, amber-dark glass. Character-forward, thumbnail-clear silhouettes. Original The Last Lithoi designs only. No publisher logos, no studio marks, no dates, no “coming soon” type. Not European bande dessinée, not photoreal, not modern gacha anime, not 3D.

## Subjects



### Key art (cast grouping)

White background. Graphic stacked poster. Front: nine-year-old Caelen, gaunt, dirty thin shift, cat-eye lantern catchlight, not holding a sword. Beside him: Varrick, older scavenger, grey-threaded trimmed beard, pack, short iron pry-bar. Behind them, large and overlapping: a costly Talon-like Shift — raptorial, torn at the joints, amber-dark marrow-glass shining dully in the chest, not a red western dragon, not feather-wings. Aspect 3:4.

### Deep strata seam

Cel-shaded environment illustration, limited palette. Mine gallery, timber, floodwater, lanterns. Wall seam of amber-dark marrow-glass with a cracked child-sized empty pocket. Graphic, not photoreal. Aspect 16:9.

### Glass Wastes

Cel-shaded badlands: extraction terraces, vitrified bottle-glass ground, ash. No ziggurat-city. Aspect 16:9.

### Lithoi reconstruction (illustrative)

Cel-shaded full figure, white ground. A person — heavy, terrene, stone-dusted skin, amber-dark marrow visible as geological inclusions *inside* the limbs, not cysts on the surface. Grief, not a golem, not a Buddha statue, no plaque text. Aspect 3:4.

### Marrow-glass / Aethel-Core

Cel-shaded still life, graphic shapes. Raw amber-dark chunk and a cut conduit shard, brass cutter, stone dust. Aspect 4:3.

### Brass conduit

Cel-shaded close-up. Forearm brass housing, three slots, one cut marrow shard locked, metal reads as brass not iron. Aspect 16:9.

### Caelen, after the seam

Cel-shaded character on white or mine-dark graphic ground. Nine-year-old boy, small, knees once-folded, thin shift, grey lips, cat-eye shine. Not a teen, not a swordsman, not large sparkle eyes. Aspect 3:4.

### Varrick, forest edge

Cel-shaded character. Not-young scavenger, grey-threaded beard trimmed neater than his clothes, pack, pry-bar, reading mud. Rain suggested with graphic strokes. Aspect 3:4.
