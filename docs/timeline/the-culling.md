# The Culling

The historical genocide and extraction event in which the **Lithoi were hunted to extinction for their marrow**.

It pre-dates the novel's present by **generations**. Its guilt structures every faction. It should surface in dialogue and setting well before anyone delivers a history lesson.

## What is established

- The Lithoi are gone. What remains of them is **Aethel-Core** / **marrow-glass** — vitrified marrow and organ tissue in the deep strata.
- The hunt was for fuel. Industry is the aftertaste, not a separate story.
- **Guild extraction**, **Synod suppression**, and **Warden enforcement** are three later answers to the same crime. None of them undo it.

## Present-day aftershocks

- Deep-strata mining is the industrial continuation of the hunt, now aimed at graves instead of people — though the Guild would not use those words.
- Unlicensed Resonance is policed as if the crime might start again from the other direction: not Lithoi returning, but humans wearing stolen marrow badly.
- Caelen is pulled from a stratum pocket no survey team charted. He is not Lithoi by blood. The Culling still sits in the amber around him.
