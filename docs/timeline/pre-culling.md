# Pre-Culling

The novel's present sits **generations after** the Culling. Pre-Culling history is therefore inherited, contested, and mostly unspent on the page — a pressure in the rock, not a prologue.

## What is established

- The **Lithoi** were a humanoid-terrene lineage. Their dense marrow conducted planetary resonance. They are extinct.
- **Lithosomatic Resonance**, in whatever undamped form it took, existed before the Aethel-Core trade hollowed it into industry. The Vault is later implied to hold that distinction (full resonance versus cut fuel).
- Human/settler societies already lived in relation to Lithoi remains and, eventually, to Lithoi bodies. The exact diplomacy, worship, or coexistence of that era is not specified.

## How this era should feel in prose

Soft, resonant Lithoi-origin words (Aethel, marrow-glass) against the hard consonants of the human world that consumes them. Pre-Culling is smelled before it is explained: amber-dark, faintly warm stone, a pulse in the deep that is not a heartbeat.
