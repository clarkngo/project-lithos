# Post-war reconstruction

"Reconstruction" here means the world the book walks through: not healed, only reorganized around the hole the Lithoi left.

The Culling is generations ago. The present is mines, doctrine, fringe towns, and badlands.

## What is established

- **The Mining Guild** owns deep-strata extraction, including the mine of Caelen's origin. Survey, blast, ledger.
- **The Warden Enforcers** police unlicensed Resonance and Aethel-Core trade.
- **The Synod** adjudicates the moral status of Lithoi remains and Resonance use.
- **Hollow Cut** — fringe settlement; found-family ground; destroyed in Act One's climax (outline).
- **The Glass Wastes** — over-mined badlands, contested enough that patrols thin out. Act Two's working ground.
- **The Vault** — sealed Lithoi repository, hidden even from the Guild according to Act Two intelligence. Contested.

## What reconstruction actually reconstructed

Industry, not the dead. Marrow-glass moved from atrocity to inventory. Resonance moved from whatever it was into a three-slot brass market. Peace, when the Synod offers it, is a lid.
