# Kell

**Role:** Mining Guild regional director — the patient appetite, as opposed to her predecessor's brute force.

## Physical description

<!-- TODO: confirm against manuscript — age, build, House/origin if any. -->

Greying early at the temples in a way that reads as a woman who has spent considerable years thinking harder than anyone around her. Dresses and travels plainly for her rank; arrives alone rather than with an entourage, as a matter of method.

## Voice / dialogue notes

Economical, honest about her price rather than concealing it — which is what makes her harder to refuse with righteous anger than her predecessor. Patient to the point of unsettling.

Reads a room, and a person, the way an Inquisitor reads a case file. Never raises her voice.

## Relationships

- **Caelen's crew** — approaches them openly in Book Two with a funding offer for the rite, in exchange for its methodology; refused, but reads the refusal as confirmation rather than defeat.
- **Custodian Voss** — their off-the-books arrangement (Guild provisioning in exchange for a licensed survey allowance on the second seal) is exposed during the Book Two rescue and becomes the scandal that ends both their careers in their respective institutions.
- **Chairman Reth** — her Board superior in Book Three; demotes her, then dismisses her outright once she testifies against his petition.

## Arc summary

<details>
<summary>Contains Book Two–Three outline spoilers</summary>

Introduced in Book Two as the Guild's new regional director, deliberately methodical where her predecessor was reckless — she reads Book One's failure as a failure of method, not of goal. Offers the camp funding for the rite; offers Rook a private, far more personal bargain (Guild physicians for Varrick, in exchange for information about the resealed first Vault) that he refuses, not easily. Her arrangement with Custodian Voss to jointly control the second seal is exposed when Caelen's crew raids it, costing her the directorship — demoted to "Auditor of Deferred Accounts."

Book Three: reappears to warn Caelen's crew about Chairman Reth's petition from the inside, then testifies publicly against her own Board at the Spire hearing — using her own failed arrangement with Voss as proof that no "modest" charter stays modest once institutions hold it. Dismissed from the Guild for the testimony. Offers herself, afterward, as the hall's own independent auditor for its new houses of treatment — "a woman who has spent her life building the hidden version might be of some use guarding the open one."

</details>
