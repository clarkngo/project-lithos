# Custodian Maren

**Role:** Twenty-nine-year-old Custodian handed the acting Eldest's chair she never sought — the first Synod voice in three generations willing to say "I do not know" aloud.

## Physical description

Confirmed on the page (Book Three, Act One, first appearance):

- Young, visibly so among the horseshoe's pale tiers. Robe cut a little large for her. Rises to speak without seeming to decide to, hands pressed flat against her thighs.
- Voice shakes when she speaks in the hall, and she lets it rather than mastering it.

## Voice / dialogue notes

Plain and direct where the doctrine trained her toward "the matter is under consideration." Admits uncertainty aloud — a small institutional sin she commits repeatedly and on purpose.

## Relationships

- **The eldest Custodian** — chooses Maren as acting Eldest specifically because she is afraid, calling fear "the only qualification I have ever trusted." Dies partway through Book Three; Maren is confirmed Eldest afterward.
- **Custodian Voss** — visits her in the records vault seeking what certainty actually costs; Voss tells her the real question is what she can bear to have been wrong about.
- **Chairman Reth** — her counterpart across the hearing; neither defeats the other cleanly.
- **Caelen** — rules, provisionally, in favor of the finding his testimony helped shape, and tells him plainly that the finding "will be tested" and that she "could bear to be wrong about it."

## Arc summary

<details>
<summary>Contains Book Three outline spoilers</summary>

Introduced as a minor Custodian thrust into the acting Eldest's chair by accident, mid-hearing, with no doctrine adequate to the moment. Opens the hall to testimony for the first time in three generations rather than letting it hear only from itself. Writes the first clause of what becomes the hall's finding — a public register, no containment without a hearing, publicly funded houses of treatment, no practice of the rite a crime — after Voss gives her the only usable wisdom either of them has: decide what you can bear to be wrong about, then act, and stay ready to be corrected.

Confirmed Eldest after the previous Eldest's death. Renders the hall's provisional finding; it passes 22–19. Ends the book presiding over an institution that has, for the first time, begun arguing with itself in public rather than behind closed doors.
