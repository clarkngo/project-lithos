# Varrick

**Role:** Veteran rogue mentor.

## Physical description

Confirmed on the page (first appearance, forest above the mine):

- Not young. Grey threads a beard trimmed with more care than the rest of him suggests he usually bothers with.
- Moves with economical caution — eyes to the ground, to the trees, to the ground again.
- Pack straps worn pale with use. Short iron pry-bar at the belt; oilcloth-wrapped cargo that sits faintly warm (marrow-glass, though Caelen has no word for it yet).
- Joints crack audibly when he straightens. He is already slower than the work he does, and he knows it.

## Voice / dialogue notes

Undercuts tension with dry, economical humor — never whimsy. His jokes are load-bearing; they exist to keep him from saying the true thing.

He does not ask twice. He sets terms, walks, and lets wanting do the rest.

Cadence is clipped rather than courtly. He will name a thing ("strata child") the way a man names a tool he has found more valuable than the job he brought it for.

## Relationships

- **Caelen** — finds him, feeds him, takes him in because he is worth more walking than reported. Teaches Resonance as survival instruction, not as magic-school lecture.
- **Rook** — trades with him in Hollow Cut; the three fall into an uneasy found-family rhythm.
- **The Mining Guild** — he is Guild-exiled, working the forest's edge for salvageable marrow-glass. The exile's cause is not yet on the page.
