## Voice / dialogue notes

Warden Enforcers speak in procedural, clause-stacked sentences — the grammar of people who have learned that precise language is what keeps them blameless later.

Malakar specifically: clinical precision that frays, almost imperceptibly, whenever doctrine and conscience collide — a stalled clause, a word chosen and then replaced. Do not underline the fraying. Let it sit in the syntax.

Avoid making him cartoonishly cruel. He does violence through paperwork and doctrine.

## Arc summary

Act One: first appearance as a controlled outside POV insert — procedural, closing in on Hollow Cut, authorized for the raid that burns it.

Act Two: senior within the Wardens, still on Resonance cases. Ordered to suppress Vault knowledge rather than enforce law evenly, which frays him further. He defects from open pursuit into a wary, transactional alliance with Caelen's crew — echoing, structurally, Varrick's own transactional taking-in of Caelen. Trust is negotiated term by term. At the climax he has to decide which side of his doctrine he actually believes.
