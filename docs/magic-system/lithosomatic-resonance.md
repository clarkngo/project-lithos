# Lithosomatic Resonance

Lithosomatic Resonance is the discipline of locking up to **three Aethel-Cores** into a **brass arm conduit** to trigger a physical transformation — **a Shift**.

It is not a costume change. It is a violent biological process with a beginning, a middle, and an aftermath. Every Shift costs something visible: torn skin, cracked nails, a tooth loosened, a voice gone raw, then the cold.

## The conduit

The conduit is the mechanical housing, typically forearm-mounted. Always brass. Always **cold before** a Shift and **scalding after**.

Three slots, in order:

| Slot | Name | Required? | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Form Vector** | Yes | Skeletal scaffold the body reshapes toward |
| 2 | **Affinity Cadence** | Yes | Elemental breath / temperament layered onto the Form |
| 3 | **Mutator Node** | Optional | Modifier that scales or distorts Form + Cadence |

Locking cores is the trigger. Unlocking them does not instantly return a body to what it was; the aftermath is paid in time, food, and heat.

The housing is the same object as the conduit: brass, forearm, three sockets. Wardens seize a **housing**. Scavengers get them reforged quiet. A core can be **near spent** and still count as a core.

**Ordinary Resonants** need core + housing + a fitting they have not lied about. **Caelen** does not. See the [Field manual](field-manual.md) for hold duration, mid-Shift swaps, licensed work, unassisted transformation, and Drift as it is actually spent.

## Aethel-Cores

Vitrified, fossilized Lithoi marrow and organ tissue, mined from deep strata and cut into conduit-ready shards. Raw, uncut material is **marrow-glass**: glassy, amber-dark, faintly warm to the touch.

Cores are fuel, relic, and contraband depending on who is holding the sentence. See [The Mining Guild](../factions/mining-guild.md), [Warden Enforcers](../factions/warden-enforcers.md), and [The Synod](../factions/the-synod.md).

## What a Shift does to a body

The Form Vector writes the scaffold. The Cadence writes the breath. The Node, if present, writes the exaggeration.

Then the bill:

- **Caloric debt** — fat and muscle burned at a lethal rate
- **Tissue tearing** — the body does not have spare room for a new skeleton
- **Post-Shift hypothermia** — violent thermal crash after the work
- **Atavistic Drift** — the mind's slow walk toward predatory instinct
