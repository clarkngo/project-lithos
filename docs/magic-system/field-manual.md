# Field manual

Writer-facing practice for **present-day** Resonance: brass housings, licensed cores, costs, Drift, and the one body that does not need any of that.

This is not a second magic system. It is how the three-slot grammar in [Lithosomatic Resonance](lithosomatic-resonance.md) is spent on the page. Pre-Culling Lithoi resonance stays out of this file except as a warning not to invent it.

Where a rule is taken from the manuscript, it is stated as fact. Working hypotheses are marked `<!-- TODO: confirm against manuscript -->`.

## Vocabulary (as spoken)

| Word | Who uses it | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Aethel-Core** | Everyone | Cut shard of vitrified Lithoi marrow; fuel |
| **Marrow-glass** | Scavengers, miners | Raw, uncut material, still warm |
| **Conduit / housing** | Everyone | Brass forearm piece with three fitted sockets. Varrick says both words; Wardens seize a *housing* |
| **Fitting** | Anyone who wants to live | Matching core, housing, and what the body can afford |
| **Shift** | Everyone | The transformation. Never instantaneous |
| **Half-Shift** | Adult Caelen, field practice | Lighting only as much of the coal as the job needs — a lengthened hand, a hauled wheel — without tipping into the hungry undertow |
| **Unassisted transformation** | Warden doctrine | A Shift with **no core, no housing, no residue**. The file has no clause for it |
| **Wild Resonant** | Wardens, Varrick teaching survival | Unlicensed, unbound; no core to confiscate |
| **Lithosomatic Resonance** | Synod halls | The full mouthful. Nobody says it twice outside those halls |

## Ordinary Resonants (everyone who is not Caelen)

A licensed or unlicensed Resonant **purchases** the means:

1. Cut Aethel-Cores (they can be *near spent* — a knucklebone shard may only be worth a meal)
2. A brass housing, fitted to the forearm
3. A **fitting** that does not lie about what the body can pay

Lock the cores. If the fitting holds, the body changes shape and what it is made of **for a while**. Unlocking does not put the old body back on a hook. Aftermath is food, heat, and time.

Warden procedure assumes this economy: **confiscate the core, seize the housing, license the conduit or destroy it.**

### How long a Shift is held

Established:

- Varrick: the change is **for a while**, not a new permanent species.
- A measured **half-Shift** can be as short as the space of a breath (a lengthened hand on a lock) or as long as it takes to haul a wheel free.
- Consecutive Shifts called up **before the last has guttered out** are how Drift gets a vote (Sinking Fields: four in a row).
- Failing to **come back** is not the same as holding on purpose. Varrick watched a licensed Talon man stay half in the shape a day, then two days, longer each time — that was Drift, not a job.

Working rule for drafts:

- A Shift is **work**, not a costume worn until bedtime.
- End it when the job ends. Staying in the shape to feel strong is how names start going.

### Swapping cores mid-Shift

Not on the page.

Working rule: **do not**. Unlocking does not revert the body instantly. Changing a Vector while the scaffold is already tearing would be rewriting a skeleton that is still occupying the meat. If a draft needs a mid-Shift change, it should cost more than starting over — and should be flagged until the prose confirms it.

### Licensed work

Established:

- Housings are mended in Guild **licensed forge-halls**, and also quietly by men who do not ask where the marrow came from.
- A **licensed housing** can run three cores locked and legal (Varrick's Talon man, a decade of salvage).
- Civilian licensed Shifts exist: a harvest-festival **Talon Vector** meant as a controlled display of strength. When the fitting fails, doctrine still has to file what is left.
- Ordinary Resonants spend **past anything a licensed core and housing can recover from** if they attempt Lithoi-scale work. That is why Caelen is the one asked to read the Vault-adjacent shard.

Warden *line-of-duty* Shifting (Malakar personally locking a triad) is still not specified on the page. Treat him as procedure first, not as a licensed combat Resonant, until the manuscript says otherwise.

## Caelen (strata-grown)

He was **grown in it**, not touched by a core. First accidental Shift: no core, no conduit, no housing, no fitting.

Adult camouflage: an **empty** brass housing on the **left** forearm, never once holding a core, acid-washed to look ordinary. A boy with no visible conduit and unlicensed Resonance is still the thing Wardens have no paperwork for.

He spends **himself** — whatever sits where a boy's marrow ought to be — not Aethel-Cores. Varrick has no ledger for how deep that well goes.

Discipline (Act Two status quo):

- Bank the coal unlit for **weeks** when the work does not call for it.
- Light only as much as the day can pay: **half-Shift** for a cart; full reach only when fear or collapse spends the vote.
- Licensed Resonants would not survive spending the way he does, **if** the spending is rationed rather than seized.

Warden file language: **unassisted transformation**; no core residue on the person.

Do not use Caelen to invent a second, cleaner system. He is the same grammar with the housing torn out. The Vault will later show what undamped Lithoi resonance was. That reveal is not this manual.

## Drift (meter, not a level)

Established:

- Synod word: **Atavistic Drift**. Varrick uses it because it is as good as any.
- Cause: Shift too far, too often, too close to the edge of what the body can afford — and, for Caelen, spending the well inside himself.
- Early signs: forgotten names, not coming all the way back, staying half in the shape longer each time.
- Wardens file the end-state as a **wild-animal incident** when they can.
- Rest and food pay **caloric debt**. They do not reverse Drift. They may **arrest** a slide if the person is still choosing to come back.
- Book Two names a **tethering rite**: Lithoi-era treatment (not a cure, not Synod suppression) for drawing a drifting mind back. Synod doctrine preferred suppression and silence.

Feral Node as an in-world Drift accelerant remains a catalog hypothesis. The *effect* (instinct arriving early) is already how Drift feels on the page.

## After a Shift (field)

Same list as [Costs](costs.md), now with the hold-rule attached:

1. Food immediately, more than seems decent.
2. Heat that does not touch brass still cooling.
3. Time before speech. Do not demand a name in the first minutes.
4. Watch whether they **come back**, or stay half in the shape.
5. Do not call a second Shift until the first has **guttered**. Consecutive lighting is how the door sticks open.
