# The Warden Enforcers

**Role:** Paramilitary order that polices unlicensed Resonance use and Aethel-Core trade. Procedural, bureaucratic violence.

## Structure

Wardens speak — and therefore act — in stacked clauses. Precise language is what keeps them blameless later. The raid, the seizure, the case assignment: violence that can be cited.

Malakar is the order as a person: an investigator, not a duelist, closing a file that happens to be a child.

## Motivations

Keep unlicensed Resonance off the street. Keep Aethel-Core trade inside channels the ledger and the doctrine can both see. "Wild" resonant children are confiscated or killed on sight — that is the survival instruction Varrick gives Caelen, not yet a quoted statute.

The Wardens' guilt is the Culling's guilt wearing a badge. Their answer is enforcement: if Resonance is going to exist, it will exist under watch.

## Relationship to the Culling

The Culling is the historical crime that made their job feel like hygiene. They do not need to be cartoonishly cruel. Paperwork is enough.

## Relationship to Resonance

Unlicensed Shifting is the offense. Licensed use is not innocence; it is paperwork that held. Atavistic Drift is feared as a public-order problem as much as a private damnation — a mind that has eroded toward predatory instinct is a Warden's future case file.

When Vault knowledge surfaces, the order's temptation is suppression: not even law applied evenly, but doctrine applied where it is told to apply.
