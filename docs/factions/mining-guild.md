# The Mining Guild

**Role:** Industrial extraction — owns and operates the deep-strata sites, including the mine of Caelen's origin.

## Structure

The Guild is an industrial body, not a church and not a state. It counts heads that go down a shaft. It does not, as a matter of practice, count the ones that come back out a different shape.

What is on the page: survey crews, blasting teams, a ledger logic that treats marrow-glass as inventory, and a willingness to sit on a collapse rather than admit what was lost in it.

## Motivations

Extraction. The richest and most dangerous marrow-glass is in the deep strata. Aethel-Cores are the fuel of all Resonance work; whoever owns the seam owns the fuel.

The Guild's guilt, like every faction's, is underwritten by the Culling. Their answer to that guilt is not refusal. It is procedure: survey, blast, cut, ship.

## Relationship to the Culling

The Culling hunted the Lithoi to extinction for their marrow. The Guild is the industrial heir of that hunt. It does not speak of genocide in the galleries; it speaks of strata, yield, and unlicensed salvage.

## Relationship to Resonance

The Guild does not exist to Shift. It exists to feed Shifting. Unlicensed marrow-glass trade (Varrick's work) is a ledger problem that becomes a patrol problem. A "strata child" is not a miracle. It is a discrepancy — something lost, something to be recovered, named, and described.

When the Vault enters the political frame, the Guild's interest is extraction rights: an untapped Aethel-Core reserve that could restart mass harvest.
