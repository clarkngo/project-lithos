## How to use this bible

- Prefer the manuscript when the two disagree. Patch this bible, not the prose.
- Do not invent major plot events, backstory, or lore facts here. Placeholder notes belong in TODO comments.
- The companion site (`apps/codex-web`) **reads these files at build time**. Do not duplicate lore into the app.

## Spoiler policy

Character arc summaries and Act Two beats are writer-facing. The Codex site collapses them behind a spoiler disclosure by default.
